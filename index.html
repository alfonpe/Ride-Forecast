        What's the “second opinion” line?

        The bold line is a second opinion: the European weather centre runs its forecast about 50 times from slightly different starting points, and this is the share of those runs that bring rain when you pass. Near 0% or 100% means the runs agree; in between, the forecast is uncertain, so pack a jacket and check again closer to the day.
